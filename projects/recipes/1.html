Grandma's Meatballs

Story and Signifigance

These meatballs, usually served with spaghetti have served an important tradition for me growing up. As kids me and my sister would go out to my Grandma and Grandpa's house in Culpeper VA, where the first night she would always make these same meatballs. They are very good and always remind me of childhood whimsy.

"The secret isn't just the basil—it's letting the meatballs simmer right in the sauce for at least two hours so they soak up every bit of flavor." - Grandma Karin
