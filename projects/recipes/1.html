Grandma's Meatballs

Story and Signifigance

These meatballs, usually served with spaghetti have served an important tradition for me growing up. As kids me and my sister would go out to my Grandma and Grandpa's house in Culpeper VA, where the first night she would always make these same meatballs. They are very good and always remind me of childhood whimsy.

"The secret isn't just the basil—it's letting the meatballs simmer right in the sauce for at least two hours so they soak up every bit of flavor." - Grandma Karin

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 25 minutes | Cook Time: 2 hours | Total Time: 2 hours 25 minutes

Servings: 6 servings (approx. 18 meatballs)

Ingredients & Instructions

Quantity / Amount Ingredient Step-by-Step Instructions
1 lb (450g) Lean Ground Beef Step 1: In a large bowl, combine ground beef, pork, breadcrumbs soaked in milk, parmesan, eggs, garlic, parsley, salt, and pepper. Mix gently until combined.
1 lb (450g) Ground Pork Step 2: Roll mixture into golf-ball-sized meatballs (about 18 total).
1/2 cup Italian Breadcrumbs Step 3: Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear meatballs on all sides until browned (5-7 minutes).
1/4 cup Whole Milk Step 4: Transfer browned meatballs into a large pot of simmering marinara sauce.
1/2 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese Step 5: Cover and simmer on low heat for 1.5 to 2 hours, stirring occasionally.
2 large Eggs Step 6: Serve hot over pasta or with fresh crusty bread.
3 cloves Minced Garlic
1/4 cup Chopped Fresh Parsley
1 tsp Salt and Black Pepper
4 cups Tomato Marinara Sauce
