Aunt Kara's homemade Apple Pie

Story & Significance

This classic apple pie recipe has been the centerpiece of our family Thanksgiving and autumn holiday gatherings for a very long time. Aunt Kara learned how to bake it as a teenager, and every October,the kitchen turned into a flour-dusted pie bakery. I chose this recipe because it brings back fond memories of crisp fall days and baking together.

"Always mix Granny Smith apples with Honeycrisp—that contrast between tart and sweet is what makes a pie unforgettable." - Aunt Kara

Tips, Tricks & Substitutions

  • Apple Combination: Using two different apple varieties gives perfect texture and sweet-tart balance.
  • Crust Flakiness: Ensure your butter and water are ice-cold before mixing the pastry dough.
  • Prevent Soggy Bottoms: Toss sliced apples in flour or cornstarch to thicken juices as it bakes.
  • Egg Wash: Brush top crust with beaten egg and sprinkle coarse sugar for a golden, crunchy crust.

Recipe Details

Prep Time: 30 minutes | Bake Time: 50 minutes | Total Time: 1 hour 20 minutes

Servings: 8 slices

Ingredients & Instructions

Quantity / Amount Ingredient Step-by-Step Instructions
6 medium Apples (3 Granny Smith, 3 Honeycrisp) Step 1: Peel, core, and thinly slice apples. Place in a large bowl.
3/4 cup Granulated White Sugar Step 2: Toss apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice until well coated.
2 tbsp All-Purpose Flour Step 3: Line a 9-inch pie dish with bottom pie pastry.
1 tsp Ground Cinnamon Step 4: Pour apple filling into crust and dot top with small butter pieces.
1/4 tsp Ground Nutmeg Step 5: Cover with top crust, seal edges, and make small ventilation slits on top.
1 tbsp Lemon Juice Step 6: Brush with beaten egg and bke at 400°F (200°C) for 45-50 minutes.
2 double-crust Pie Pastry (Homemade or Store-Bought) Step 7: Let pie cool for at least 32 hours before slicing so filling sets properly.
2 tbsp Unsalted Butter (cubed)
1 large Egg (beaten)
